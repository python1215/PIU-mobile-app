Performance Benchmarks (World Bank Standards):
  • Excellent: ≤15% NRW (Best practice)
  • Good: 16-25% NRW (Acceptable level)
  • Moderate: 26-35% NRW (Needs improvement)
  • Poor: >35% NRW (Critical action required)
Improvement Strategies:
  • Implement systematic leak detection and repair programs
  • Upgrade aging water distribution infrastructure
  • Install pressure management systems
  • Improve water meter accuracy and billing systems
  • Conduct regular water audits and loss assessments
  • Train staff on water loss management best practices
