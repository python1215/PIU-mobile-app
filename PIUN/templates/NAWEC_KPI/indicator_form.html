Indicator Guidelines
Best Practices:
  • Unique Numbers: Use consistent numbering (KPI001, KPI002)
  • Clear Descriptions: Be specific about what is measured
  • Measurable: Ensure the indicator can be quantified
  • Relevant: Align with project objectives
Examples:
KPI001: Percentage of households with access to clean water
Attributes: Percentage, Quarterly
EFF001: Energy efficiency improvement rate
Attributes: Percentage, Annual
Important Notes
  • Indicator numbers must be unique across the system
  • Once created, indicators can be used in monitoring data entry
  • Consider future reporting needs when defining indicators
  • Coordinate with team members to avoid duplicates
