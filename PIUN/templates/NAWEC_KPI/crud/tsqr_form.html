TSQR Calculation

TSQR measures the timeliness of quarterly report submissions. Formula: TSQR = (B ÷ A) × 100 where A = Due Date (days) and B = Actual Date (days)
A value ≤ 100% indicates on-time or early submission, > 100% indicates late submission.
