About KPI for Contracts:

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for contracts help track and measure the effectiveness and progress of project activities and deliverables.

  • Monitoring Code: Unique identifier for tracking and reporting
  • Investment Type: Links KPI to specific funding streams
  • Description: Clear definition of what is being measured
  • Project Link: Associates KPI with specific project outcomes
  • Monitoring Type: Defines the measurement methodology
