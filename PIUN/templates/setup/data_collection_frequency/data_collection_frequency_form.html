About Data Collection Frequencies:

Data collection frequencies define how often project data should be collected, monitored, and reported to ensure effective project tracking and evaluation.

  • Daily: High-frequency monitoring for critical activities
  • Weekly: Regular progress tracking and short-term indicators
  • Monthly: Standard reporting cycle for project milestones
  • Quarterly: Comprehensive reviews and major assessments
  • Semi-annually: Mid-year and year-end evaluations
  • Annually: Long-term impact assessment and planning
  • Ad-hoc: Event-driven or as-needed data collection
  • Real-time: Continuous monitoring for automated systems
