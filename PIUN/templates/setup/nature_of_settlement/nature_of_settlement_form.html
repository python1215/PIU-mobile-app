About Nature of Settlement:

Nature of settlement defines the method or type of compensation provided to Project Affected Persons (PAP) for their losses or displacement.

  • Cash: Monetary compensation for losses
  • Land: Replacement land of equal value
  • Land + Cash: Combination of land and monetary compensation
  • In-kind: Non-monetary compensation (materials, services)
  • Relocation: Assisted relocation to new location
