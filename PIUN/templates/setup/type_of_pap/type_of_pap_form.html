About PAP Types:

Project Affected Persons (PAP) types help categorize different groups of people who are impacted by development projects.

  • Aggrieved: Persons who have experienced direct negative impacts
  • Non-Aggrieved: Persons affected but without significant grievances
  • Vulnerable: Persons requiring special attention (elderly, disabled, etc.)
  • Host Community: Local communities hosting project activities
