About Decision Outcomes:

Decision outcomes represent the final result or status of decisions made during project implementation, grievance resolution, or approval processes.

  • Approved: Decision or request has been approved
  • Rejected: Decision or request has been denied
  • Pending: Decision is still under review
  • Closed: Issue has been resolved and closed
  • Escalated: Decision has been escalated to higher authority
  • Withdrawn: Request has been withdrawn by applicant
