About Indicator Types:

Indicator types classify performance measures by their purpose and level in the results chain, helping organize monitoring and evaluation frameworks.

  • Input: Resources invested in the project (funding, staff, materials)
  • Output: Direct products or services delivered by the project
  • Outcome: Short to medium-term effects on beneficiaries
  • Impact: Long-term sustainable changes and benefits
  • Process: How activities are being implemented
  • Quality: Standards and satisfaction measures
  • Efficiency: Cost-effectiveness and resource utilization
  • Coverage: Reach and accessibility of services
