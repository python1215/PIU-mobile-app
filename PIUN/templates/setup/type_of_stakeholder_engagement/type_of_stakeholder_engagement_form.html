About Stakeholder Engagement Types:

Stakeholder engagement types define the various methods and formats used to consult with and engage project stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle.

  • Community Meetings: Large group consultations with local communities
  • Public Hearings: Formal public consultation sessions
  • Focus Group Discussions: Targeted discussions with specific groups
  • Key Informant Interviews: One-on-one consultations with leaders
  • Surveys/Questionnaires: Structured data collection from stakeholders
  • Workshops: Interactive sessions for collaborative planning
  • Site Visits: Field-based consultations and demonstrations
